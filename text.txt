What is RAG ?
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) in LLMs is a technique that enhances Large Language
Models by connecting them to external knowledge bases, allowing them to retrieve relevant, up-to-
date info before generating answers, making responses more accurate, factual, and domain-specific,
like giving an LLM an "open book" for answers instead of just relying on its internal training data
